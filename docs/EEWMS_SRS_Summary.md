# EEWMS SRS — Configuration Item Summary

**Source:** EEWMS Software Requirements Specification supplied for this project.  
**Purpose:** SCM-friendly summary for this Experiment 9 repository. The full controlled SRS remains the authoritative source.

## System purpose

The Employee Engagement & Wellness Management System (EEWMS) is intended to support employee engagement, workplace wellness, communication, recognition, and employee experience through a centralized platform.

## Principal users

- Employees
- Team managers and supervisors
- Human Resources (HR) personnel and wellness coordinators
- Counselors or partner providers
- System administrators and IT support

## Main functional areas

- Pulse surveys and employee feedback
- Peer recognition and wellness challenges
- Mental-health resources and counseling support
- Manager one-on-one meetings and action plans
- Engagement scoring, sentiment indicators, and HR analytics

## Key quality and control considerations

- Role-based access to sensitive features and records
- Privacy-aware aggregation that protects small employee groups
- Security and confidentiality of wellness-related information
- Reliable capture of survey responses and clear submission feedback
- Human review of analytics and sentiment indicators rather than treating them as definitive employee judgments
